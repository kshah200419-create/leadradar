## Project description (what it does, who it helps, how it uses SerpApi)
LeadRadar is a freelance lead-finder powered by SerpApi's Google Maps engine.
Type a business category and city — e.g. "cafe", "Vadodara, Gujarat" — and get
a ranked prospect list scored on real outreach signals: missing website, missing
phone, reputation pain (low rating x high review count), review volume, and
premium price band. Each prospect lands in a pricing tier mapped to a service
offer (PRO Rs.14,999 / GROWTH Rs.7,999 / NURTURE), with a one-line "why" per
lead and one-click CSV export of the outreach sheet.

Who it helps: freelancers and small agencies who sell services (web design,
marketing, automation) to local businesses and currently build prospect lists
by hand, hours at a time.

How it uses SerpApi: meaningfully, not decoratively. The Google Maps engine
(engine=google_maps, type=search) is the product's only data source — every
listing's title, rating, review count, price band, address, phone, website, and
GPS coordinates comes from a live SerpApi response. The scoring engine turns
that search data into the core decision the product exists for: who to pitch,
at which price. No search data → no score → no product. Includes an offline
fixture mode so the scoring logic is testable without API credits.
